# Research

My research focuses on embodied agents and safe, reliable agents. I am especially interested in systems that can operate safely and effectively in realistic environments while supporting people through personalized assistance.

## Interests

- **Embodied Agents**: planning in 3D environments, memory, and personalization for human assistance.
- **Safe & Reliable Agents**: risk-aware decision making, trustworthy human-agent interaction, and agent evaluation.

## Current Direction

At Yonsei University, I work with Prof. Jinyoung Yeo on embodied-agent planning and evaluation. My recent projects include hazard-aware guardrails for safe planning, personalization through memory utilization, fine-grained evaluation of text-guided 3D scene synthesis, and direct evaluation of harness optimizers.

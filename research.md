# Research

My research focuses on AI agents, AI safety, and embodied agents. I am especially interested in building and evaluating agent systems that can operate safely in realistic, personalized, and constrained environments.

## Interests

- **AI Agents**: agent systems for web, AR, and embodied environments.
- **AI Safety**: hazard-aware guardrails and direct evaluation methods for agent optimization.
- **Embodied Agents**: memory utilization, planning, personalization, and fine-grained scene constraint verification.

## Current Direction

At Yonsei University, I work with Prof. Jinyoung Yeo on agent evaluation and embodied agent systems. My recent projects include hazard-aware guardrails for safe planning, personalization through memory utilization, scene-description alignment in text-guided 3D scene synthesis, and privacy-constrained multi-agent collaboration.

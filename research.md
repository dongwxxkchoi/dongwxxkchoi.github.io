# Research

My research focuses on building human-centered AI agents, particularly embodied agents that interact with and assist people in complex environments.

## Interests

- **Embodied Agents**: planning in 3D environments, memory, and personalization for human assistance.
- **Safe & Reliable AI Agents**: safe autonomous agents, trustworthy human-agent interaction, and agent evaluation and benchmarking.

## Current Direction

I pursue this vision along two complementary directions: enabling context-aware and personalized assistance through planning and memory, and developing safe and trustworthy agents through research on autonomous-agent safety and human-agent interaction. Across both directions, I explore agent training, evaluation, and benchmarking to improve and rigorously assess their capabilities and reliability.
